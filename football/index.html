The team that finishes first in the conference standings is the champion. Teams tied for first are co-champions, and the automatic bid (AQ) goes to the winner of the tiebreakers: 1) head-to-head among the tied teams; 2) point differential against the tied teams; 3) fewest defensive yards against the tied teams; 4) random draw.
